Size Guide

Measure once, save it here, and every order starts with your numbers filled in. Saved on this phone only.

My measurements

Use a soft tape, in centimetres or inches, whichever you prefer. Be consistent.

How to measure

Chest / Bust: around the fullest part, tape level under the arms.

Waist: around the narrowest part, above the belly button.

Hip: around the fullest part of the hips and seat.

Shoulder: across the back, bone to bone.

Sleeve: shoulder tip to wrist with the arm slightly bent.

Length: from the base of the neck to where you want the hem.

Foot length: stand on paper, mark heel and longest toe, measure the gap.

How It Works

Ordering on CUDWEARS takes four simple steps, and you pay the way that suits you.

1. Find a design

Browse by category, search for a style or visit a designer. Tap the heart to save designs for later.

2. Make it yours

Pick up to six colours (the photo previews them), choose a size, tick modifications and add your measurements and notes. Ordering several? Each one can be different.

3. Pay the deposit

Place your order, get a tracking ID, then pay the deposit by OPay, PocketApp or bank transfer and send the receipt on WhatsApp. Nothing is charged online.

4. Track & receive

Follow your order under Track while it is made and shipped. Pay the balance before or on delivery.
